# Privacy Policy

**EmPoster** ("the app") is an on-device utility that lets you manage wallpapers and system appearance tweaks on your own iOS device.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is:

- No analytics
- No advertising
- No tracking
- No cloud accounts or servers of our own

All processing happens locally on your device.

## Device Access

The app only reads and modifies files on your own device (for example, wallpaper/PosterBoard data and the MobileGestalt cache) that are required for the features you choose to use. Nothing from your device is sent anywhere unless you explicitly perform an action such as an in-app login through the app's account provider (for example, Patreon), which is governed by that provider's own privacy policy.

## Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed for its features. You can revoke permissions at any time through iOS Settings.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the app owner via the project's GitHub repository.
